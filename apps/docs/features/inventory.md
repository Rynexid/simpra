# Inventory

Inventory management lets you track every item across warehouses and locations.

## Key capabilities

- Item master with SKU, category, and unit of measure
- Stock levels per warehouse and zone
- Stock movements (inbound, outbound, transfer)
- Approval workflow for high-value adjustments
- Analytics by category, movement type, and period
